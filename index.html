A comprehensive, future-ready Class 1 to Class 10 training framework mapping Artificial Intelligence, Next-Gen Robotics Coding, and Strategic Digital Marketing Frameworks.

Class-Wise Master Syllabus (1 - 10)
Classes 1 & 2 Early Tech Foundation

🧠 Artificial Intelligence

  • Identifying smart devices around us
  • Basic difference: Human vs Machine intelligence
  • Fun activities with Google Quick, Draw!

🤖 Robotics & Coding

  • Intro to directions & basic computational step logic
  • Block-based visual block arrangement (Scratch Jr)
  • Understanding inputs (sensors/buttons) & outputs (lights)

📢 Digital Literacy / Marketing

  • Introduction to digital screens & safe device handling
  • Visual communication: Expressing ideas through icons
  • Recognizing patterns and brand logos
Classes 3 & 4 Primary Innovators

🧠 Artificial Intelligence

  • How machines see: Introduction to Computer Vision basics
  • Smart Assistants (Alexa, Siri) & voice command responses
  • Introduction to data classification patterns

🤖 Robotics & Coding

  • Creating interactive animations in Scratch (Loops, Sounds)
  • Virtual Robot navigation through basic mazes
  • Introduction to Motors and Gears concept

📢 Digital Literacy / Marketing

  • Understanding the concept of Internet & Websites
  • Digital Citizenship: Cyber safety & netiquette
  • Basic presentation skills using images and graphics
Class 5 Intermediate Thinkers

🧠 Artificial Intelligence

  • Introduction to AI ethics and decision making
  • Games based on Machine Learning (Teachable Machine)
  • Understanding Data Collection & Data Privacy

🤖 Robotics & Coding

  • Using conditional statements (If-Else) in block coding
  • Introduction to microcontrollers (Micro:bit / Arduino block simulators)
  • Designing a smart light automation block model

📢 Digital Literacy / Marketing

  • Introduction to Digital Content Creation
  • How Search Engines work (Google search tips & tricks)
  • Basics of Graphic Design using online canvas tools
Class 6 Middle School - Level 1

🧠 Artificial Intelligence

  • The Three Pillars of AI: Data, Computer Vision, and NLP
  • Introduction to Data Science & Spreadsheet data manipulation
  • AI Project Cycle Phase 1: Problem Scoping

🤖 Robotics & Coding

  • Transition to text-based logic concepts
  • Circuit design basics: Resistors, LEDs, and Power Supplies
  • Simulating line-follower robot logic in Tinkercad

📢 Digital Marketing

  • What is Digital Marketing? Traditional vs Digital Channels
  • Introduction to Social Media Platforms & Audience reach
  • Designing eye-catching banners and posters for online presence
Class 7 Middle School - Level 2

🧠 Artificial Intelligence

  • Introduction to Natural Language Processing (Chatbots, Translators)
  • AI Project Cycle Phase 2 & 3: Data Acquisition & Exploration
  • Basics of Python Programming Language Syntax

🤖 Robotics & Coding

  • Working with Sensors: Ultrasonic, IR, and Temperature sensors
  • Arduino programming basics using simulation tools
  • Project: Building a distance-alert smart obstacle model

📢 Digital Marketing

  • Basics of Content Marketing & Copywriting principles
  • Email Marketing fundamentals and building a newsletter
  • Introduction to Blogging & Website creation basics (CMS)
Class 8 Middle School - Level 3

🧠 Artificial Intelligence

  • Introduction to Machine Learning Frameworks & Neural Networks
  • Python Programming: Conditionals, Loops, and Basic Lists
  • AI Evaluation metrics: Understanding accuracy & system feedback

🤖 Robotics & Coding

  • Introduction to Bluetooth/Wi-Fi modules (ESP32 / IoT basics)
  • Integrating multiple sensors and servo motors together
  • Project: Designing a Smart Dustbin or Automated Home system

📢 Digital Marketing

  • Introduction to Search Engine Optimization (SEO) & Keywords
  • Understanding Video Marketing & basic video production tricks
  • Web Analytics basics: Traffic, Clicks, and Bounce Rates
Class 9 CBSE Code 417 Aligned

🧠 Artificial Intelligence

  • Comprehensive AI Project Cycle (Full framework management)
  • Advanced Python: Functions, NumPy array environments
  • Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) integration with AI solutions

🤖 Robotics & Coding

  • Embedded C / Advanced Python controller scripts
  • IoT integration with cloud data dashboards (ThingSpeak/Blynk)
  • Project: Real-time environment monitoring weather node station

📢 Digital Marketing

  • Social Media Marketing (SMM) strategies & ad campaigns
  • On-Page and Off-Page SEO optimization pipelines
  • Understanding E-Commerce architectures and digital gateways
Class 10 CBSE Board Exam Ready

🧠 Artificial Intelligence

  • Advanced Computer Vision architectures & NLP deep pipelines
  • Data Science: Advanced analytics with Pandas and Matplotlib
  • Deploying functional Machine Learning models on local frameworks

🤖 Robotics & Coding

  • Autonomous system engineering: Navigation & calibration scripts
  • Hardware optimization, troubleshooting, and power diagnostics
  • Capstone Project: Completely operational IoT/Robotic Smart Framework

📢 Digital Marketing

  • Search Engine Marketing (SEM) & Pay-Per-Click (PPC) ads setup
  • Advanced Digital Analytics, ROI calculations, and growth funnels
  • Capstone Project: Designing an end-to-end launch strategy for a brand
